# Browser release 1.31.1

Universal-jurisdiction endorsements with explicitly agreed limits and sovereignty safeguards, or a state-developed consensual normative basis, are qualified support. The rule retains source wording and an explanatory qualification. A compatible support expression does not turn qualified support into a conflict; explicit support and opposition on the same question/date remain mixed. Qualified support stays outside same-day and nearby exact-direction comparisons.

The country briefing, overview, daily evidence tables, trajectory tooltips, JSON and source qualifications distinguish qualified support from conflicting positions. Collection records and filters are unchanged; analysis regenerates the correction on reopening. No country-specific override or new score is introduced.

Validation: synthetic qualified/unqualified/support-opposition cases, reported and collective attribution, dated separation, duplication invariance; browser save/reopen and script-free export with qualified-support wording; private Nigeria source review kept outside the repository. Reserved meetings and publication gates remain unchanged.
