## New temporal-dependence estimator and synthetic calibration

**Estimand:** signed **scalar** year-aggregate contrast `beta=+0.20` on a synthetic speech-language feature. This tests temporal covariance and sampling design; it does **not** estimate the magnitude or significance of the actual W4 64-dimensional Euclidean movement.

- Synthetic country trajectories: **99 recorded affiliations** over **T=8,16,24** years (4/8/12 per era), with each year assigned 12 synthetic meeting groups and source sampling in four chronological meeting-number quartiles. Synthetic future years are NOT real original-UN-source events.
- Random model: `y_it = beta*post_t + a_i + u_t + m_{g(i,t),t} + epsilon_it`. Country intercept `a_i ~ N(0,0.40²)`, meeting-within-year shock `m ~ N(0,0.12²)`, idiosyncratic speech noise `epsilon ~ N(0,0.25²)`; the common year effect `u_t` has stationary marginal SD **0.20**, with either iid draws `rho=0` or stationary AR(1) `rho=0.65`. Third world adds response/inclusion probability depending on observed synthetic lexical residuals (MNAR; an explicit *violated ignorability* stress test). Actor, meeting, year and residual innovations are independent before that response mechanism.
- Two synthetic masks: **near-census** (all 12 synthetic meetings, with exactly two fixed synthetic missing actors per year), and **two-stage original-PV-like** (in early-era years select two meetings out of each chronological meeting quartile, eight of 12, with exactly known `p=2/3`; later-era years near census; in the MNAR stress, additional response selection depends on generated outcome). Meeting assignments vary deterministically by actor/year and random seed; never synthesize a verified historical source record.
- Year summaries: within each year form the **Hájek ratio** of observed synthetic speech outcomes using **inverse recorded meeting inclusion probabilities**. Record year-level effective sample sizes `n_eff=(sum w)^2/sum(w²)`, and the fraction of missing/dropped records. Do not claim these weights solve unknown actual UN source nonresponse.
- Temporal covariance family: `Cov(z) = sigma²[(1-lambda)R_AR1(rho) + lambda*diag(99/n_eff_year)]`, where `R_AR1(rho)_{ts}=rho^|t-s|`. Both `rho` and nugget share `lambda` are **estimated by profile restricted maximum likelihood** over a prespecified grid `rho ∈ {-0.15,0,0.15,0.30,0.45,0.60,0.65,0.75,0.85,0.90}` and `lambda ∈ {0,0.1,0.25,0.50,0.75,1}`; estimate `sigma²` by the REML residual quadratic `SSE/(T-2)`. Report serial correlation bias/RMSE, nugget selection, boundaries, failures and not merely CI coverage. No true covariance parameter may be supplied to an estimator.
- **Three nominal 90% interval competitors:** (A) unweighted year-mean Welch t interval, (B) feasible grid-REML estimated-covariance GLS with approximate `t_(T-3)` reference, and (C) a **fixed covariance-family envelope**: union/hull of GLS `t_(T-2)` intervals over the prespecified grid (no plug-in oracle covariance, conservative but potentially very wide). The envelope is a **finite-grid robustness diagnostic, not a proven 90%-honest interval for actual UN data**; this grid need not contain the actual covariance or missingness mechanism. All intervals target the known scalar `beta`; failures and sizes are explicit.
- Independent synthetic generator and calibration evaluator: separate modules for (1) frozen Gaussian source/year/actor DGP and (2) calendar-year covariance fitting. Verify null beta=0, seeded replay, shuffled country-label invariance, source-probability accounting, likelihood/profile parameter recovery on a known covariance fixture, and withholding if fewer than two sampled meeting sources in a preregistered quartile.
- Monte Carlo **R=300** per scenario; **T=8/16/24 × two masks × three worlds = 18 scenarios = 5,400 attempted panels**. Master simulation seed **20261013**. Nominal 90% criterion: at least 285/300 evaluable intervals, absolute coverage gap ≤0.04 and Wilson 95% interval containing 0.90; median interval width also reported, with width >0.8 flagged impractically broad. A **temporal covariance estimator is point-reliable** only if absolute mean rho bias ≤0.10, RMSE ≤0.25 and fewer than 5% grid-boundary selections for rho over a scenario. No post hoc grid/cutoff alteration after observing results.
- Explicit tests should reveal lower coverage under serial-year or informative selection if dependence is misspecified. Neither high coverage from an excessively wide envelope nor synthetic success with true nuisance parameters grants actual W4 confidence intervals.

## Source and publication acceptance gates

- Independent source sampling *frame* PASS is not independent source *bytes* PASS; independent original-PV text verification per sampled speech is a subsequent gate. Do not announce source population expansion until encrypted complete PDFs have been retrieved and individually checked against the published Harvard v14 text, with denominator/count ledger.
- Even authentic 2008–2015 text may have different document/editorial/language conventions from 2016–2023; compare source text QA and fix the historical representation training window before any combined-year real-data fits. Do not refit the existing frozen 2016–2019 representation and label it a 2008–2023 reference.
- Simulations cannot establish true year covariance of actual UN General Debate: real eight-year W4 source selection and year shocks remain unquantified, observed country/source-speaker shifts are confounded, and only one speech per country per year is typically present. **No real confidence interval, year-cluster p-value, national alignment, governmental policy change, browser publication, or new D1 release approval.**
- Original Harvard ZIP/source hashes, holdout evaluations, 24 owner reviewer decisions, all 37 reserved 2026 meetings and public site remain unchanged. Public GitHub may store only reproducible code, selected official meeting symbols, synthetic tests, preregistration and aggregate receipts; never private source-linked speaker rows/PDFs.

**Stop rule:** If covariance estimation fails at least one serial-year design at any annual horizon, classify real-world temporal inference **WITHHELD**. Do not tune the estimator on the same synthetic calibration scenarios to manufacture a pass.
