## B. Temporal-dependence model

**Known-truth synthetic scalar estimand:** equal-recorded-affiliation difference between two equally long eras, true shift `beta=0.20`, no actual diplomat text or stance. Annual observations indexed by actor `i`, year `t`, original meeting `g(i,t)`. DGP:
`y_it = alpha_i + beta*post_t + u_t + m_{g,t} + epsilon_it`,
with `alpha_i~N(0,0.4²)`, meeting shocks `m~N(0,0.12²)`, residual `epsilon~N(0,0.25²)`, year shock marginal SD `0.20`, under (i) iid year noise and (ii) stationary AR(1), `rho=0.65`. The actual 24-year source masks and PV meeting mappings are fixed **before** simulated outcomes and may be nonrandom; a separate deliberately informative missingness stress is required and cannot be treated as MAR.

**New source-crossed covariance estimator:** remove persistent actor offsets by a deterministic within-actor projection `H`. On the resulting 24 annual moments define covariance `Sigma = sigma_y² G R(rho) G' + sigma_m² F F' + sigma_e² H H'`, where `G` aggregates cross-year shared shocks and `F` groups speech observations by complete original meeting. All design matrices are built from the **observed** source-mask/meeting/actor map; the singular overall level is projected into the `T−1` contrast subspace. Estimate meeting and observation variance from source- and actor-/year-demeaned residual moments, **not true simulator parameters**. Estimate year variance and rho by bounded **REML profile** on the reduced annual moments, with rho search in [-0.85,0.85], source/year variances constrained positive, and exact finite-sample likelihood/diagnostic failures. Original meeting/year source IDs are never treated as independent country rows.
- New intervals: **(A) plug-in source-crossed REML GLS** using a preregistered conservative Student-t `df=max(2,T−5)`; **(B) studentized parametric bootstrap** with **B=99** model-estimated-nuisance synthetic draws, fully re-estimating covariance for each bootstrap draw, reporting failures and withholding interval if fewer than 90 complete draws. Never supply generating rho, true meeting/observation/year variances or the known true beta to the estimator.
- Comparators from 12 October: naive country-paired t, Welch on year means and previously implemented one-dimensional feasible AR(1) REML, run without tuning from their historical state. The new covariance method estimates **meeting and year** dependence together, unlike scalar annual AR(1).
- Declare estimator failure on nonidentifiable variance, rho boundary hit, nonconvergent REML, singular contrast covariance, empty actor era, too few independent calendar blocks or missing original source unit. Report all attempted/delivered/prescreen-rejected and reasons.

## C. Independent calibration plan

- Source design years: **8, 16, 24**. Primary new test is **24 annual blocks**; 8 and 16 are explicit stress/comparator designs. Two era splits of 4/4, 8/8 or 12/12 respectively. Use actual approved source-verified country/year/meeting mask by year after source revalidation. For synthetic additional/non-observed source opportunities, **do not fabricate source-certified speeches**; restrict to each actual accepted mask or a separately labeled artificial repeat-mask stress whose design is declared, retaining all source ID uniqueness.
- **Development (method debugging only):** R=120 and seed 20261013, reporting tuning/exclusions explicitly; this stage is not acceptance evidence. Freeze executable code and method settings after development.
- **Independent validation:** R=400 per combination, new nonoverlapping seed **20261113**, two year worlds (iid vs AR1 rho=.65), two masks (full source-attested source-year mask and predetermined six-PV balanced subset), 3 year lengths (8,16,24), **12 independent scenarios** = 4,800 attempted panels. Use no data-derived changes after inspection. For each estimator/scenario report accepted/failed draws, 90% coverage, Wilson 95% interval, Monte Carlo SE, median interval width, rho bias/MAE/boundaries and variance-component diagnostics, plus coverage failures from informative-source censoring in a **separate** preregistered stress experiment (not mixed into the primary 12).
- Calibration criterion for the synthetic DGP: ≥380/400 evaluable, empirical coverage within ±0.04 of 0.90, Wilson 95% interval contains 0.90, and ≤5% covariance estimation/interval failures. Report results by every scenario, not only passing T=24 cells. **Reliability claim** at T=24 additionally requires passing both iid and serial-year worlds under both source masks (4/4), no large systematic rho bias (|mean rho − true rho| < 0.15) and no mislabelled source/actor independence. Passing single scenarios is *limited synthetic-DGP evidence only*.
- Do not claim any empirical interval for actual 2016–2023 W4 distances, new 2000–2023 linguistic movement, country political positions or policy alignments. Real-world inference remains **WITHHELD** until source comparability, selection, meeting/year dynamics, feature-fit uncertainty and prospective external validation pass separately.
