# W4 Joint Transcript-Selection and Temporal-Dependence Model — Locked Preregistration

**Protocol frozen:** 13 October 2026, before the new two-phase model implementation, pilot tuning or independent holdout simulation. This is a **new** research experiment; it does not replace, tune retroactively, or invalidate the already completed October 12/13 calibration failures. No actual UN General Debate inferential interval is authorized.

## Research decision and identification

Observed national addresses are selected twice: (S) an original UN PV meeting is included in an archival sample, with known recorded inclusion probability `pi_{meeting}`; (R) the text or speaker attribution survives corpus extraction and full-original-PV verification. Even if `pi` is known, `P(R|Y, source, year)` is **not identified from verified texts alone** when R depends on their latent language outcome Y. Source inventory and token matching rates alone do not identify selection on unobserved text. A model fitted to verified speeches without randomly audited nonresponders therefore must **abstain** from a real-world interval, regardless of its synthetic performance.

The new design explicitly requires independent second-phase **randomized verification of otherwise missing/failed speech outcomes** at known probability `q=0.40`, with all source failures and unverifiable outcomes recorded. Random audit assignment is set before observing the missing speech outcome, independent within source/year selection strata; audited outcomes must be obtained from original approved UN source/PV text on the same frozen representation. If the audited outcome cannot actually be recovered, it must remain missing rather than fabricated.

## Synthetic estimand, sources and DGP

Target is a **known signed scalar period effect beta = +0.20**, *not* the observed 64-dimensional W4 country distance and never a political position. Data are **99 fictional affiliation trajectories** in `T = 16, 24, 32` equally spaced years (T/2 per era), each distributed deterministically among **12 distinct synthetic PV meeting families per year**; one speech per affiliation/year. No real UN country, meeting text, reserved 2026 source, actor identity or 2008–2023 derived vector enters the numerical synthetic experiment.

Generate `Y_it = beta * post_t + A_i + U_t + M_{meeting(i,t),t} + epsilon_it` with `A_i ~ N(0,0.40²)`, meeting-within-year shock `M ~ N(0,0.12²)`, individual residual `epsilon ~ N(0,0.25²)`; `U_t` is stationary Gaussian with marginal SD 0.20, either independent (`rho=0`) or AR(1) (`rho=0.65`), with independent innovations SD `0.20*sqrt(1-rho²)`. Actor, meeting, year and residual innovations are mutually independent. Neither the true rho, variance components nor beta are supplied to an estimator.

Meetings undergo one of two **outcome-independent** first stages: `near_census` all 12 meetings, or `stratified_2_of_3` exactly two meetings drawn without replacement from each of four chronological meeting-number quartiles (eight of 12), conditional first-stage `pi_m=2/3`. Fix a deterministic mapping of actors to meetings and an independent random missing **two actors per year** as a recorded measurement frame gap; those excluded actors do not enter the meeting frame denominator. The same exact source mechanism applies independently across years.

For each included speech draw transcript response R by independent `Bernoulli(expit(alpha + gamma * (Y_it - beta*post_t) + delta*post_t))` with `alpha=1.20`, `delta=-0.30`; outcome dependence is `gamma=0` for `iid_MAR` and `AR1_MAR`, and **gamma=-1.25** for `AR1_MNAR_audited` and `AR1_MNAR_no_audit`. `Y_it-beta*post_t` is defined by the generator only. The estimator has no access to beta or gamma. For audit worlds, independently and without outcome access draw `V_it ~ Bernoulli(q=0.40)` **only for R=0**; observation is `R=1` or `R=0 and V=1`. The no-audit MNAR world has q=0 and is explicitly **nonidentifiable**.

## Joint estimator (locked)

1. **Selection component:** fit a weighted logistic likelihood for `P(R=1 | Y, post)` on ordinary verified responders and independently audited nonresponders. Audit-phase inclusion weights are 1 for responders and `1/q` for audited nonresponders, multiplied by `1/pi_m` for meeting-stage sampling; estimate intercept, outcome coefficient gamma and period coefficient. Use bounded/ridge-stabilized logistic MLE with fixed L2 penalty `1e-5`, maximally **30 Newton iterations**, tolerance `1e-7`; report separations, numerical failures, coefficients, and gamma bias/RMSE, including the known gamma=0 case. Do not confuse a good fitted gamma with identification from the unverified records that lack randomized audit.
2. **Outcome component:** separately for every year estimate the source-weighted response fraction `p_R,t` from all first-stage included rows; estimate `E[Y|R=1,t]` from responders and `E[Y|R=0,t]` using inverse-`q` weighted independently audited nonresponders. Form the stratified Hájek year mean `p_R,t * mu_R,t + (1-p_R,t)*mu_NR,t`. If any year has no auditable response stratum, **fail that simulation** and do not invent a mean. This is a two-phase design-based correction of outcome-dependent response under explicitly randomized audits, not a claim that logistic selection is identified without a second phase.
3. **Temporal component:** use the complete vector of the T selection-corrected year means and effective sample sizes `(sum weights)^2/sum(weights²)` to profile-REML estimate `rho ∈ {-0.20,-0.10,0,0.10,0.20,0.35,0.50,0.60,0.65,0.75,0.85,0.92}`, and temporal nugget share `lambda ∈ {0,.20,.40,.60,.80,1}` for `Sigma = sigma²[(1-lambda) R_AR1(rho) + lambda * diag(99/n_eff)]`. Estimate sigma² from the observed GLS restricted residual quadratic; report all boundaries and failed covariance shapes. The optimizer must **never** receive true rho, gamma or covariance variance.
4. **Intervals:** nominal 90% feasible plug-in GLS Student `t_(T-3)`; and one independently **trained** simulator-calibrated t-multiplier learned exclusively from the **development simulations**. For each T, learn the 90th percentile of `abs(beta_hat-beta_true)/estimated_SE` from the pooled identifiable development worlds across both meeting masks; take the maximum of that quantile and the default `t_(T-3)` cutoff. One scalar cutoff per T, no peeking at worlds during heldout acceptance. The multiplier corrects conditional synthetic undercoverage only; it is **not** a data-identified true UN variance or a guaranteed selection-robust confidence interval.
5. **Without randomly audited missing text (q=0 and gamma ≠0),** mark source-selection parameters and interval **WITHHELD** in every synthetic replication, not a failure-free artificial fit. The actual UN P2 original sample has **no independently randomized phase-II nonrespondent outcome audit**; real-data CI remains withheld until this prerequisite is met, regardless of synthetic performance.

## Independent development and sealed test

**Development only:** `D=120` known-truth synthetic panels per identifiable (T,mask,world), seed namespace `UNGA81-JOINT-DEV-20261013`. This set may calculate the locked per-T t-multiplier and inspect diagnostics, but cannot change grids, thresholds, outcomes or acceptance after the test is opened. Development simulations are not counted as independent calibration evidence.

**Independently written holdout generator:** `H=240` panels per scenario, seed namespace `UNGA81-JOINT-HOLDOUT-20261014`, implemented separately from the development generator and validated against frozen moment/sample-frame checks. **T ∈ {16,24,32} × source design 2 × selection/year worlds 4 = 24 scenarios, 5,760 attempted holdout panels.** Identifiable worlds: `iid_MAR, AR1_MAR, AR1_MNAR_audited` (18 scenarios); nonidentifiable: `AR1_MNAR_no_audit` (6 scenarios). No calibration multiplier tuning, scenario removal, seed substitution or choice of covariance grids after inspecting these holdout results.

**Locked acceptance:** On each identifiable scenario, ≥228 of 240 completed intervals, empirical coverage within ±0.05 of 0.90, and the Wilson 95% binomial interval contains 0.90. For the estimated serial rho, require absolute bias ≤0.12, RMSE ≤0.25, and grid-end boundary rate <5% on every identifiable scenario. On MNAR-audited scenarios, fitted selection gamma requires absolute bias ≤0.20 and RMSE ≤0.45 with <5% failure. In no-audit MNAR every replication must **abstain** (report expected refusal separately; not considered a calibrated interval). Report all attempted, successful, failed/refused, coverage, median and 90th percentile interval width (flag median >0.80 as impractically wide), year-effective-N, nuisance estimates and selection model failures; don’t equate overcoverage to nominal calibration.

**Stop rule before real representation fitting:** Only consider a newly frozen source-comparable real 2008–2023 representation if (a) **all six T=16 identifiable holdout scenarios** pass coverage and rho reliability; (b) all MNAR-audited outcomes pass selection-gamma diagnostics; (c) actual 2008–2015 and 2016–2023 original-PV metadata, source modality, translations, recorded affiliations, inclusion/response probabilities and missingness have an independently verified comparable source population; and (d) any missing source outcomes used to identify MNAR are covered by an actual independently randomized, source-verified audit. If **any** gate fails, **do not fit** a new historical real-data representation in this task. Deliver a declared blocked/not-run status with the prereg code and source preflight, rather than quietly fitting anyway.

## Privacy, immutable boundaries and decision

The selected **64 original UN PVs from 2008–2015** cover 828 unambiguous source-corresponding country-year candidates from a 118-meeting index frame, but have additional source-name/duplicate uncertainties. The separately authenticated 2016–2023 record covers 1,543 historical speeches under a *different* original source-selection plan. The existing 2016–2019 TF–IDF/LSA 64-D representation is frozen; **do not** relabel a refit or a revised vocabulary as that checkpoint. Original-PV text-source comparability and selection nonresponse are not automatically demonstrated by textual overlap.

No 37 reserved October 5–6, 2026 transcripts, old source hash records, 24 human review decisions, shared analytic v1, W1 numerical kernels, publication/site code or D1 O1–O5 gates may be touched. Public GitHub accepts only synthetic model/test code, preregistration and **aggregate-only** source/computation receipts. Real source rows, original PV PDFs, speaker-linked evidence, nonrespondent texts, country-specific vectors and any private heldout outcomes stay outside the repository.

**Interpretation:** calibrated coverage on synthetic signed beta—even if achieved—does not establish calibrated uncertainty for the real W4 Euclidean representation or the source population. National positions, political movement, causation and publication remain separately withheld.
