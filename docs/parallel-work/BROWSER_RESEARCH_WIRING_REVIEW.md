## Draft active browser entry

- `site/research.html` links to the **accepted isolated W5 and W6 modules**
  with same-origin local previews, clear synthetic/engineering warnings,
  source inspectability and no hidden public-source collection.
- `site/research-wiring.js` imports **only native synthetic W3 outputs**:
  SHA256.json, interchange-v1.json, receipt.json, source-linked-edges.json
  and fit-geometry.json. It verifies all derived bytes with Web Crypto
  SHA-256 before parsing, the W5 and W6 complete-frame validators,
  W3 source/hash basis/selection, original ID/text SHA and parent/meeting
  edges, Gaussian-kNN distance/affinity, graph policy and original
  source-ordered spectral assignments. No numeric refit occurs.
- W6's corrected 24 eligible + 1 unavailable case renders the 24 original
  nodes with frame 25, excluded 1 and missing 1. Graphs above 50
  nodes display a withheld record; no subsetting is performed.
- Changing input files immediately clears old analyses and displays.
  Invalid checksum, mismatched source geometry and altered source keys
  all refuse rendering instead of reusing prior figures.
- `site/index.html` and `site/latent.html` add an explicitly experimental
  research navigation link; no original analysis control is changed.
- `tools/build_pages.py` uses an explicit, minimal **allowlist** for
  W5/W6 reviewed runtime assets and one synthetic fixture. It does not
  recursively publish worker tests, previews, review data, screenshots

## Interpretation and privacy

stability is not statistical significance. Gaussian affinity is not
cosine similarity, observed interaction, government stance, diplomatic
alliance, political influence or policy diffusion. Missing/unavailable

other servers; the W3 importer is synthetic-only and fails closed on
unverified original sources or schemas. W5's separately authorized local
