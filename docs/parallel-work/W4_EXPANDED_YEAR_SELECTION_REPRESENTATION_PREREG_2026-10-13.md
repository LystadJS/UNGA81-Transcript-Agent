## 1. New estimand and time scope

- **Primary NEW era contrast:** 2008–2015 versus 2016–2023, eight full calendar-year General Debate sessions per era. This measures long-run *representation-defined differences in General Debate language*; it is NOT an expanded post-2020 treatment effect, and cannot be used to infer one. A 2020-centered result is withheld because the comparable post-2020 interval still has only 2020–2023.
- **Primary fixed actor population:** the *already frozen 99 recorded country affiliations* in the 2016–2023 P2 restricted pilot. Do not replace these with a new complete-case population after data inspection. Use a separate, explicitly denominated prospective member-state universe for any broader census; do not assert that the UN index is an authenticated 193-member-state census.
- **Unit:** one canonical source-authenticated General Debate national address per recorded affiliation and year, after deduplication/version resolution. Track all competing texts and multiple speeches separately. The expected *candidate* grid is 99 × 16 = 1,584 country-years, not 1,584 verified addresses. Every source-unverified cell stays in the inventory as unknown, never a speech absence or zero vector.
- **Secondary sensitivity only:** 2012–2017 versus 2018–2023 (six + six years, overlapping but separate estimand); 2016–2019 versus 2020–2023 retained only as unchanged legacy descriptive control. No data-driven choice of favorable breakpoints, speaker-person attribution, policy-effect interpretation, or M24 change point.
- **Period/genre:** only original official PV-authenticated General Debate delivered-text genre using consistent English text-version and translation rules. Restrict or separate original-language, official translation, prepared-text, OCR and ASR modalities. Do not pool Harvard 2024/2025 transcription modalities into the primary population.
- **Statistical target:** expected country-equal, year-equal difference under a stipulated repeated-year/source superpopulation; separately report finite observed-corpus descriptives. Historical years are not literally random draws; dependence-model inference is conditional on explicit stationarity/trend assumptions.

Let x(a,y) be the vector of a single verified affiliation-year speech in the *same* reference-fitted 64-D lexical geometry. When multiple eligible interventions exist, take meeting-equal then genre-equal within year before the year-equal average. Let mu(a,e) be the mean of eligible year-standardized vectors in era e, with declared source-selection correction, and preserve the originally fixed 99-actor denominator or withhold the target. Declare before fitting:
- **Primary nonlinear contrast D:** (1/99) × sum over the 99 affiliations of norm_2(mu(a,post) - mu(a,pre)).
- **Secondary centroid contrast C:** norm_2((1/99) × sum over affiliations of [mu(a,post) - mu(a,pre)]).
Both are high-dimensional descriptive language measures. Neither is a signed causal effect, actor stance, voting alignment or independent-speaker motion. Report actor-year coverage and no-interpolation denominators beside each statistic.

Preserve all failed and excluded keys and reason codes.

**Source sampling and registration:** target the entire eligible original-PV universe, not merely the most accessible meetings. If full original retrieval is infeasible, precommit a source-meeting-level, stratified random supplemental audit before inspecting its document texts, stratifying by year, index/metadata quality, language/translation and prior access status. Inclusion probability pi_meeting and deterministic SHA256 sorting seed 20261013 must be recorded for all eligible meeting symbols, including never-retrieved ones. Existing purposeful P2 selection has no known design pi; do not invent one. Explicitly separate (i) document retrieval opportunity, (ii) original Harvard/PV source-text verification, and (iii) speech-in-index observation; conditional inclusion products are valid only when each factor has a documented sampling mechanism or justified model.

**New future audit split:** within each pre-2016 year, rank verified index meeting symbols by SHA256 of concatenated fixed namespace 'W4-EXT-AUDIT-20261013', year and official PV symbol. Reserve the lowest 20% of *whole meeting symbols* (minimum one per nonempty year) from any model selection, dictionary tuning, propensity optimization or calibration adjustment. This is an independent *source-authentication quality audit*, NOT an independent temporal/inferential test or a substitute for the existing 37 sealed October 2026 meeting transcripts. Register the complete symbol roster and its digest privately before document access; audit strata and exclusion reasons in a separate private ledger. Full corpus inference can be recomputed after audit is frozen with the same predeclared methods, never tuned to withheld outcomes.

**Inclusion adjustment candidates:** known sampling pi_meeting where applicable, times an estimated probability q(a,y) of successful speech verification conditional on *pre-outcome* metadata only. Use year/source-family group-separated cross-fitting or a predeclared simple logistic model; no fitting on speech contents or later outcomes to predict missingness. A stabilized Hájek/inverse-probability weighting contrast may be computed only if metadata support the assumed missing-at-random selection process. Clip inverse verified-inclusion weights at 10 as a preregistered numerical guardrail, and report clipped fraction, effective weights, min support probability and sensitivity with cap 5/20. Positivity below 0.10 for more than 5% of target cells, >5% clipped weights, or material imbalance of measured pre-selection metadata (maximum absolute standardized difference >0.10) fails selection-support eligibility; do not declare an IPW-repaired census.

**MNAR sensitivity:** missing speech may depend on unobserved lexical content. Define deterministic directional shifts of missing expected vectors along the common-era contrast direction with magnitudes delta in {-0.50, -0.25, 0, +0.25, +0.50} standardized observed within-year vector-scale units (if direction norm is zero, use a preregistered fixed first-coordinate unit direction). Report the union of identification/sensitivity intervals; it is NOT a 90% confidence interval in the absence of justified missingness bounds. A model assuming MAR must explicitly abstain from claiming identifiable inference in MNAR simulation worlds.

**Minimum empirical coverage before any inferential fitting:** 16 separate authenticated calendar-year blocks, >=95% overall verified candidate cells, >=90% verified in each year, >=90 of the fixed 99 affiliations with >=6 verified years in each era, no unexplained complete missing year, and full private roster/genre/meeting/translation registers. These are necessary descriptive adequacy screens, NOT a proof that 8 + 8 years suffice for time-series inference.

## 3. Temporal, meeting, affiliation and selection dependence

The source hierarchy is year -> original PV meeting -> delivered speech; recorded affiliations recur in and across years. Do not count 1,584 speeches, 99 country labels, passages, or 105 historical PVs as exchangeable independent year replications. Year-wide shared agenda/venue shocks may be serially correlated; source-family identities must be the original PDF, and no artificial cross-era paired meeting family may be constructed.

The predeclared variance approaches, each retaining all variance-estimation uncertainty, are:
1. **REML-like hierarchical time-series covariance:** country random effects, meeting nested-in-year intercepts, a common calendar-year AR(1) innovation with rho estimated (not supplied as truth), and idiosyncratic speech effects, potentially supplemented with predeclared smooth linear year trend. Fit parameters on the *observed sample* with parameter bounds and identifiability/convergence diagnostics, not 99 pseudoreplicate years. Form uncertainty using full parametric bootstrap with REML/AR estimation repeated in each bootstrap replicate; the contrast is recomputed from the same downstream pipeline.
2. **Year-block time-series resampling:** contiguous circular/stationary whole-calendar-year blocks independently within each eight-year era, mean block lengths 2 and 3 as fixed alternative settings; all nested PV meetings and the full multi-affiliation source bundles move together. Additional whole-meeting sensitivity is nested within years, never treated as an independent year draw. Track missing actor-periods per replicate, matched-denominator retention, fit failures, and confidence-interval non-delivery. Block-bootstrap percentiles are candidate methods, not presumptively valid intervals for T=8.
3. **Conservative sensitivity envelope:** profile a priori rho in {0, 0.35, 0.65, 0.85} and nonrandom-source delta values; report the union of conditional interval sets, widths and implausibly unbounded results. A nonparametric or partially identified envelope is NOT called a frequentist confidence interval without full relevant known-truth simulation coverage.

No feasible procedure may borrow true generating-process rho, the oracle covariance, a true speaker identity, or an unobserved source outcome. A held-out fitted rho is not external ground truth. Estimator ranking uses *all* predeclared simulation cells, not the best-performing cell; conservative oversizing, zero-delivery and empty intervals count as failures where specified.

## 4. Frozen lexical fitting, training uncertainty and alignment

The **new primary** common reference is trained *only* on the eligible 2008–2015 original delivered-text development training population, using a separately versioned, fully specified TF–IDF tokenizer, stop-word list, normalization, vocabulary, IDF and 64-D truncated SVD. Transform all 2016–2023 observations using that saved reference; never refit a comparison-period vectorizer or change embedding checkpoints between eras. Lock deterministic library/build versions, vocabulary, training ID/text/source hashes, exact selection fingerprint, training years, random seed, singular values and sign convention. Require rank >=64 and finite L2-normalized vectors; fail closed otherwise. The older 2016–2019-trained 64-D reference is an explicit *legacy descriptive comparison only*; do not overwrite it or attribute numerical differences to policy change.

Two distinct uncertainty reports are required:
- **Conditional on fixed fitted representation:** vary verified historical source selection and temporal/meeting effects, holding this exact reference checkpoint fixed.
- **Including reference-estimation uncertainty:** within each outer year/source-family resample, rederive training-only TF–IDF IDF/vocabulary/SVD64 (never using later-period text to train the basis), then project both eras into that *same resampled basis* and recompute D and C. Compare distribution and failure rates with fixed-reference results. A scalar Euclidean norm *within a single fitted basis* is invariant to coordinate sign/orthogonal rotation; an independent Procrustes map is NOT required across replicates for this scalar. Feature drift across refits and anchor instability must still be reported, and synthetic known-truth calibration must account for changing fitted vocabulary. For cross-fit maps or externally interpreted axes, use separately selected independent anchor documents, full-rank/singular-value, condition-number and relative residual checks; absence of stationary anchors means **NOT RUN**, not an identity claim.
- A pinned external semantic checkpoint may be investigated as a sensitivity branch only; do not silently replace lexical W4 or pool embeddings with lexical distances.

Representational stochastic truth must be defined independently in the simulator: an auxiliary, independent large synthetic reference population defines the population vocabulary/IDF/SVD reference and true population D and C. Each Monte Carlo observed sample retrains the declared reference and is compared to this truth. A scalar first-axis toy effect, a fixed already fitted true-basis value, or agreement with the saved 2016–2023 lexical model is NOT evidence of calibrated 64-D reference-refit uncertainty.

## 5. Prospective synthetic known-truth calibration plan (NOT RUN on registration)

**Protocol frozen before running:** 99 fictional affiliation labels; 16 annual blocks split 8+8; fictional PV meeting groups nested in years; 64-dimensional latent lexical signal mapped to a synthetic vocabulary of 1,024 word types (tokenization/count model documented before full calibration); 600 Monte Carlo datasets PER SCENARIO, 199 resampling/parametric-bootstrap draws per evaluable dataset. RNG seed 20261013, with independent deterministic scenario/replicate substreams. Two true motion settings (null and a preregistered nonzero period shift magnitude 0.20), plus pure roster-only and pure genre-only false-drift controls. Common year shock SD 0.20, meeting shock SD 0.12, recurring affiliation intercept SD 0.40 and observation residual SD 0.25; country and meeting effects are not treated as independent units for time inference.

**Factorial main grid** (36 core cells, 21,600 Monte Carlo worlds): 
- Calendar dependence: iid year (rho=0), AR(1) rho=0.65, stress AR(1) rho=0.85.
- Selection: source-complete; missing-at-random (~85% retained, with verification propensity driven by pre-outcome year/language/source metadata); missing-not-at-random (~85% retained, including additional log-odds coefficient -0.8 for unobserved standardized language-shift direction). Intercepts selected deterministically from the synthetic source frame to target the approximate retention fraction; no post hoc outcome-driven intercept tuning.
- Representation: fixed population-fitted 64-D reference versus resampled training-only TF–IDF/SVD64 refit (always both periods projected into one basis).
- Signal: no actual movement (zero) versus known movement (0.20).
- **Legacy negative control:** separate 4+4-year versions, explicitly exploratory and NEVER pooled into the 8+8 empirical calibration claim.

If the synthetic population truth cannot be computed in the same representation space, label that cell NOT RUN rather than comparing incompatible scalar targets. Preserve software/runtime/seed, source-mask rule, all failure counts, empirical bias, interval width, realized inclusion, rho estimate and convergence for *every* world.

**A procedure passes synthetic calibration for a proposed estimand ONLY IF**:
(i) >=570 of 600 evaluable intervals in each applicable 8+8 census/MAR setting; 
(ii) unconditional empirical two-sided 90% coverage in [0.86, 0.94] AND its Wilson 95% coverage interval includes 0.90 in every applicable dependence/signal/representation cell; 
(iii) actor/year/meeting/fit and selection denominators reconcile, leakage and roster-only/genre-only false-motion tests pass; 
(iv) source-selection sensitivity results and no-outcome leakage withstand held-out source verification; and 
(v) the method **abstains from identified 90% inference in MNAR settings** unless explicitly justified identification bounds containing the true target across the declared delta sensitivity set. Do not tune interval cutoffs, dependence classes, or inclusion cut points after reading coverage. If all candidates fail, inference FAIL, not "best available PASS".

A simulation PASS alone does NOT establish exchangeability or transport from fictional PV speeches to real observed years. The empirical eligibility gate additionally requires source authentication, 16 observed blocks, source/genre/selection audits, plausible stationarity, fit provenance, anchor conditions only if maps are used, and independent human interpretation review before any public release. No statement here relaxes D1 O1–O5 or approves deployment.
