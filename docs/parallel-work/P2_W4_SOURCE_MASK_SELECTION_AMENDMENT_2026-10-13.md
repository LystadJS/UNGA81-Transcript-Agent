## Preregistered source-design implementation clarification (13 October 2026)

**Logged after verifying original source bytes and match masks, but before generating any new simulated outcome or inspecting new covariance-model coverage.** This amendment fixes the deterministic actor-cohort rule that the original protocol left implicit; it is not a revision to the known-truth simulation process, estimator, test threshold or planned temporal scenarios.

For each target period `T∈{8,16,24}`, take the final T consecutive *real* source-authenticated historical years ending 2023. Apply the previously committed **six-PV ordinal-quantile selection** separately to every calendar year's original source-index PV universe, before any simulated outcome. Retain only country affiliation identifiers with **at least two unique verified source-backed country-year records in each T/2-year era** under that six-PV balanced mask. This defines an identical set of recorded country affiliations for the corresponding near-complete/full-PV source mask and balanced-PV source mask, preventing confounding from different participating countries when comparing the two masks. Include every otherwise source-valid country in that identified cohort; no subjective or source-text-based ranking, human speaker inference or imputation of unobserved years.

**Actual source-mask inclusion counts (metadata/byte verification only, no outcome):** T=8 → 82 affiliations, T=16 → 160 affiliations, T=24 → 187 affiliations. The source-year counts are 2000–2015 source-verified 237 of 239 original UN PV meeting files and previously accepted/reauthenticated 105/105 original 2016–2023 PVs. Across 2000–2023 the source audit independently finds **4,535 unique original-PV-corroborated country-year opportunities**, conditional on indexed country/meeting mapping, with genuinely missing/unverified records separated. This is not a generalizable UN member-state census.

The 2000–2015 official speaker-index PV source selection was committed before retrieval; exactly two old PV endpoints could not deliver a valid complete PDF and therefore remain source-unverified. Different recorded speakers within a country-year are never treated as two independent country-year trajectories. Original UN source-verification inclusion is conditionally based on available official documents and text fidelity, not a probability draw; simulation's fixed source mask represents that selection mechanism but does **not** prove real missingness ignorability.

**No interval calibration results existed when this rule was committed.** Continue the main preregistered estimation/calibration and failure accounting without altering this selected actor population after seeing synthetic coverage.
