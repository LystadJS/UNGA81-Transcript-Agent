# Release 1.27.0 — national implementation priorities

Reports now recognize bounded national treaty priorities and climate-finance appeals, plus three domestic implementation questions: restoring an Ombudsman, developing a child-protection strategy, and a 100% renewable-energy transition target by 2050. A numerical target requires its immediately preceding national climate-policy declaration; both sentences remain in the evidence.

Domestic implementation contexts remain distinct and are never scored as U.S. agreement. Treaty accession priorities retain their qualification; none establishes completed ratification. Collective delivery, foreign-country statements, reported language, isolated targets and historical implementation remain excluded by these additions. Original source text and saved filters are unchanged.

Validation: synthetic policy and attribution regressions, prior policy catalogue regressions and the country-comparison browser harness. The private national-source replay is a development check, not independent accuracy validation or human-confirmed annotation. The 37 reserved meetings remain unopened. No private transcript fixture is published.
