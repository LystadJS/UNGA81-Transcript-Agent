# Browser release 1.29.0

Nearby-date shared-priority coverage now includes climate finance, Convention against Torture accession, national human-rights institutions and renewable energy, alongside child protection. Explicit bounded national declarations and existing source-linked commitment rules supply evidence. Reported descriptions, completed acts alone, other treaty objects, collective attribution and subnational roles remain gaps. Qualified domestic plans retain their qualifications and never establish exact-policy agreement.

Each area shows dated country/U.S. evidence coverage and its limits automatically. Missing evidence is not neutrality. Shared priorities retain signed date differences and the saved window, remain distinct from exact-question direction comparisons, and do not change same-day scoring. Exact comparisons are not repeated as broad priority matches.

Validation: synthetic object-specific positives and negatives, national attribution, conditional/reported/implementation abstention, repetition invariance, source-reviewed grammar patterns, browser save/reopen and export checks. These are engineering checks, not a general accuracy estimate or human validation. Reserved meetings remain unopened; no external AI calls or publication-gate changes.
