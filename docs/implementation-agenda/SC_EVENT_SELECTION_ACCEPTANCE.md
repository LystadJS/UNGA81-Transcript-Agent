## Selection policy

For an eligible adopted match, select one DDLrepresentative and retain the complete DPPAevent as alternative evidence. This does not turn DPPAunverified unanimity into affirmative ballots: the two sources keep separate modalities, categories, eligibility and review notes. DDLunknown/non-voting records remain unknown. Source-only valid adopted events retain an explicit coverage gap; failed drafts keep separate native action identities and source outcomes. Nonparticipation notes remain separate from ballots.

Reference, roster, ballot, participation, duplicate-identity and same-resolution/different-date conflicts withhold the affected action. Date ambiguity is withheld even when only one source contains the competing records. Malformed references and failed outcomes carrying adopted-resolution references cannot become verified separate actions. Stale crosswalks are rejected through exact recomputation and canonical full-input fingerprints, including provenance-only changes. The optional wrapper pins the selector dependency and writes a private derived artifact atomically.
