# Evaluation contract v1

This contract separates executable engineering checks from empirical acceptance. No fitted trajectory estimator or accepted empirical benchmark ships with this change. `site/issue-models.js` provides source-bound panels, exact observed comparisons, assessment gates and reproducible artifact identities. The candidate registry marks state-space, smooth and dynamic-vote methods as not implemented.

## Estimands and units

Statements: the categorical explicit national position on one policy object in one context on a particular date. Support, opposition and qualified support remain distinct; conflicting same-day expressions remain conflicting. The statistical unit is a country/policy/context/date cell. Repeated passages do not add weight. Original events and source revisions remain attached. Missing dates have no imputed stance. Collective attribution, unresolved wording, missing provenance and unspecified contexts cannot enter national comparisons.

The observed reference compares only clear support/opposition on the same policy, context and exact date. The U.S. reference changes on its own observed dates. Shared direction is narrower than exact policy agreement. No country statement is carried forward. A future modeled reference must propagate uncertainty from both countries, with estimates visibly separated from observations.

Votes: the recorded vote on the same adopted resolution version and date, with known eligibility. Yes, no and abstention are separate categories. Absence, ineligibility, unknown votes, consensus records, amendments and procedural votes cannot be recoded as substantive votes. Vote alignment and statement positions have separate denominators and tournaments. Resolution-to-policy binding needs its own source-backed review before issue-level modeling.

## Evaluation and abstention

Training, validation and final test periods must be chronologically disjoint. A source group represents the full meeting/document family whose dependent fragments must remain together; groups cannot cross splits. Related translations, revisions, repeated interventions and resolution stages need upstream grouping. Owner-confirmed labels are required for empirical acceptance; assistant-provisional labels are useful for development only. Split IDs, source grouping policy and adjudication state are frozen and versioned before candidate comparison. Never inspect the reserved 37 meetings as development inputs.

Candidates must predict the same registered estimand on every eligible test item. The implemented assessment checks complete probability vectors, categorical prediction sets, proper scoring rules (log loss and Brier score), confidence-bin calibration error, prediction-set coverage, runtime and separately supplied stability diagnostics. Initial engineering gates require 30 test items, five countries, five distinct dates and five held-out source groups; Brier at most 0.5, calibration error at most 0.15, coverage between 0.85 and 0.99, runtime at most 10 seconds and stability delta at most 0.1. These thresholds are provisional configuration, not a claim of diplomatic validity. Their substantive adequacy and subgroup performance need owner review before empirical release. Scalar modeled alignment intervals will require additional interval-coverage and recovery assessments; categorical prediction-set coverage does not validate them.

Eligible candidates rank by held-out log loss, then Brier score, runtime and stable identifier. Incompatible estimands cannot compete. No accepted candidate produces an observed-evidence baseline with visible gaps. The assessment function does not authenticate benchmark labels or run fits: the calling pipeline must supply provenance-validated, genuinely held-out predictions, measured runtime and preregistered perturbation diagnostics. Synthetic tests validate gate execution only. They cannot establish classifier accuracy, model calibration or generalization.

## Reproducibility and invalidation

Artifacts freeze source snapshot, ontology, extractor, panel, method and evaluation versions; as-of date, full historical window, scope, policy/context, parameters; assessments; observations and versioned source dependencies. Deterministic canonical keys include all these fields. Keys are identity strings, not cryptographic integrity signatures. Source corrections invalidate dependent artifacts; unrelated source changes do not. A future authoritative store must also invalidate transitive dependency chains, including newly inserted matching evidence not previously present, through snapshot changes. Reopening reads the frozen artifact. Refreshing creates a new identity.

## Remaining gates

No source-backed fitted candidate is presently accepted. State-space and smooth candidates need an owner-confirmed benchmark, enough asynchronous historical evidence, source-dependence checks, simulation recovery, interval coverage and out-of-time evaluation. Dynamic vote models additionally require verified resolution policy bindings and historical eligibility. Browser delivery, empirical model assessment and scientific release acceptance are separate milestones.

## Executable experimental candidates

`site/issue-candidates.js` implements two bounded exploratory distributions for the same categorical statement target: exponentially discounted past categories with a Dirichlet prior, and a one-sided Gaussian kernel over past categories with the same prior. These are recency-weighted evidence baselines. The first is not a fitted latent state-space process; the second is not a GAM. Accordingly, these functions do not complete the scientific E3/E4 estimator milestones or replace the observed report. They are explicitly experimental, unaccepted and uncalibrated.

Training uses only dates strictly earlier than the prediction date and no later than the frozen cutoff; forecasting past that cutoff is disabled. Country, policy and context must match exactly. Unknown contexts, collective attribution, unresolved records, official documents and missing source revisions are excluded. Same-day conflicting categories are withheld. Each distinct date counts once regardless of repeated passages. Defaults require three training dates, effective recency weight of one and a latest observation within 180 days; these are provisional gates whose scientific suitability needs assessment. Event and parameter limits bound work.

The comparison function computes both national and changing U.S. category distributions, retains qualified probability mass and withholds output if either side lacks support. Its product distribution explicitly assumes conditional independence. It produces neither a confidence interval nor a calibrated probability of political agreement. Synthetic checks demonstrate category recovery, recency sensitivity, future/test-date exclusion, source/context separation, duplicate invariance, conflict handling and long-gap withholding. They do not demonstrate empirical accuracy, uncertainty calibration, or model selection readiness. Candidate predictions may enter a future same-estimand tournament only after actual owner-confirmed benchmark evaluation; the main report continues to show observed comparisons.
