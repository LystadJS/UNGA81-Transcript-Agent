Research-development status remains restricted research for the product; no historical product permission is inferred.

Membership validation does not recompute the original private-index-dependent design or frame digests, infer selection probabilities, or merge the two designs. Authority comparisons use explicitly scoped LF-normalized metadata hashes in memory, while retaining raw input fingerprints. The original historical files and their byte-hash receipts remain unchanged. Code-authority hashes are matched declarations against trusted configuration; the builder does not independently authenticate those code bytes. The recorded year is the frozen General Debate roster's session-start grouping year, not a general UN meeting-date rule or an exact speech date.

This module does not modify or silently enable the earlier source register: an explicit later adapter and independently supported permission metadata are required before expansion.
