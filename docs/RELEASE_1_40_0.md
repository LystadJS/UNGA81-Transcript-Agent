# Release 1.40.0

This release repairs the deterministic day-summary engine using the finalized three-day owner-reviewed development sample.

- Literal action fragments now require a complete policy object or remain withheld.
- Reviewed wording routes to named policy questions, while broad catalog subjects split into narrower measures.
- Document editing, credentials, speaking limits, floor requests and meeting scheduling remain outside substantive summaries.
- Collective statements retain their group identity and cannot enter national participation counts or country-topic cells.
- Proposal and disagreement cues exclude reviewed biographical, reported and duplicate-action constructions.

The same 12 registered development meetings and 439 retained source segments were replayed without opening the reserved 5–6 October meetings. Literal source-derived topics fell from 84 to zero; 57 of 59 owner-marked topic misrepresentations route to named questions. All four reviewed erroneous broad-topic merges split, and 20 of 21 reviewed collective-attribution errors retain collective identities; the remaining source is withheld as non-substantive.

These are development diagnostics. The repaired output has not received a new owner review and does not establish acceptance. The untouched test remains sealed. See [the repair record](DAY_SUMMARY_DEVELOPMENT_REPAIR_2026-11-05.md) and [method](DAY_SUMMARY_METHOD.md).
