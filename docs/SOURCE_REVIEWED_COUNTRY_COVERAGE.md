# Source-reviewed country coverage · 1.23.0

The retained October 14–16 collection was audited for the 112 country-affiliation rows without an identified position under 1.22.0. The private audit records source IDs, hashes, candidate expressions and national-attribution availability. This is assistant-provisional engineering review, not owner-confirmed annotation or a representative accuracy/recall benchmark. Original transcript text and source-bearing audit files remain private. The 37 reserved October 5–6 meetings remain unopened.

The main repair recognizes direct self-declarations using the exact recorded country name or a bounded declared alias, plus `my country`, `our country` and national delegation/government wording. Present-tense support, opposition and commitment are recognized; courtesy prefixes and limited discourse adverbs do not hide them. An Armenia-affiliated record saying China supports a treaty still cannot become an Armenian position. Historical accession/ratification and past support do not automatically establish a current position. Quoted, reported, collective, institutional, ambiguous and unresolved evidence remains withheld. Direct policy-object obligations such as a treaty `must be respected` are recognized; descriptions of promoting proposed wording in a past process do not establish a direct present obligation.

Two-state expressions concerning Gaza, the West Bank and Israel/Palestine use a common Israeli–Palestinian context. This repair applies only to that policy question; humanitarian-access evidence in Gaza and the West Bank is not merged. Other regional contexts and unknown-location abstention remain in place.

Seven source-supported additions bring the catalogue to 87: respect for international humanitarian law, poverty eradication, food security, the Secretary-General good-offices mission in Cyprus, the Global Alliance for Human Rights, cooperation with civil society, and ending Israeli occupation of the Syrian Golan. Cyprus and Golan questions require their declared contexts. Spelling variants of Nonproliferation are accepted. Optional Protocol support is not silently treated as support for the underlying Convention against Torture.

Rows without results now distinguish attribution withheld from a failure to identify a supported explicit expression. Neither means neutrality or absence of a position. Ambiguous proposals, pronoun-only commitments with no clear policy object, conditions on the ground and historical acts are not filled in. Source summaries and provisional country attribution remain separate from verified government policy or implementation.

Comparisons still require explicit matching question/context/date U.S. evidence; repetition adds no weight and missing dates are not carried forward. All original records, hashes, recording notes, exclusions, saved controls and meeting groups retain their existing persistence. Country results are regenerated locally from those preserved inputs.

Tests cover recorded-country support/opposition, conditional and possessive commitments, exact affiliation boundaries, reported/past/question abstention, collective attribution, policy obligations, family-specific context handling, the seven additional questions, source excerpts unchanged, duplicate-invariant weighting, browser exports and exact save/reopen recovery. No external AI calls.
