# Browser release 1.32.0

Country briefings separate UN statements, executive policy, congressional analysis, enacted U.S. law and other official evidence. The documentary section and script-free exports use the same categories. Historical/dated status and comparison qualifications remain visible beside the source, date and signed date difference.

Categories derive conservatively from recognized official source locations, not issuer prose, document age or political direction. Unknown and international institutional sources remain separate. Old saved collections regenerate the sections without a migration; validated documentary JSON includes the derived evidence category. No sources, filters, scores, trajectories or attribution rules change.

Validation covers category isolation, unknown and misleading host abstention, historical dates, visible gaps, unchanged scoring, source binding and saved collections. Documentary data and private Nigeria review artifacts remain outside the public repository. Reserved meetings and analytical gates are unchanged.
