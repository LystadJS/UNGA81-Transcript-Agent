An explicit position and an admissible comparison are separate. A recognized regional position with no reliable geographic context is retained as “Context not established.” It never establishes agreement or opposition to the U.S., even if both countries use identical wording on the same date. Ambiguous multiple locations and unspecified treaty instruments also remain comparison gaps. The country overview, trajectory tables, script-free HTML and CSV expose the missing context. A country can therefore have a source-supported expression while having no usable comparison.
