# Policy coverage · 1.22.0

Every meeting or collection report automatically checks 80 explicit policy questions across eight areas. It remains country-first: no policy or country dropdown is required, and evidence volume does not determine importance. The report includes an expandable policy-area coverage table showing questions checked, questions identified and countries with identified positions. The catalogue below it is grouped by area. CSV now includes `policy_area`; saved originals and filters retain their existing format.

The 50 additions cover mine and cluster-munition treaties, conventional weapons, nuclear-test moratoria, no-first-use commitments, negative security assurances, outer-space arms control, autonomous-weapons negotiations, UN peacekeeping, the UN Cybercrime Convention, the 0.7% ODA target, sovereign debt restructuring and cancellation, concessional finance, social protection, technology transfer, illicit financial flows, international tax cooperation, WTO and Doha commitments, adaptation finance, transition away from fossil fuels, renewable energy, biodiversity and BBNJ agreements, plastic-pollution negotiations, Sendai and early-warning systems, gender equality, Women Peace and Security, women's participation in peace processes, ending child marriage, torture and disappearance conventions, refugee and migration frameworks, disability rights, UN80, veto restraint in mass atrocities, African permanent Council seats, ICJ, UNCLOS, human-rights-based AI governance, universal health coverage, release of all hostages, Libyan elections, withdrawal of Russian forces from Ukraine, extraterritorial application of the Cuba embargo and lifting unilateral coercive measures.

Contexts now include Libya, Somalia, the Democratic Republic of the Congo and Western Sahara. Country-specific questions declare allowed contexts. Libya elections cannot become a Sudan comparison; a passing Cuba mention cannot turn a Gaza blockade into a Cuba position. Regional questions with no unambiguous context remain gaps. Global questions describe the expressed direction on a stated international objective or named instrument, not agreement on every national programme, amount, implementation condition or institutional decision.

Debt cancellation is distinct from restructuring. The ICC and ICJ are distinct institutions. A refugee compact is not a migration compact. Security Council expansion or African permanent seats does not automatically establish every form of Council reform. Ending extraterritorial application of the Cuba embargo does not itself establish support for ending the whole embargo. Lifting unilateral coercive measures requires an explicit lifting/removal/ending object and a regional context; supporting sanctions does not establish support for lifting them. Object terms such as `unilateral` do not themselves make that question conditional, but actual if/unless/only clauses still do. Decimal ODA targets and U.S. abbreviations survive local action/object matching.

Coverage is broader, not universal. Descriptions, quotations, group positions, unknown wording and unsupported policy objects still cannot establish a national position. Same-question/context/calendar-date U.S. evidence is required. No carry-forward or interpolation. One question-date has equal weight regardless of repeated statements. Related questions are not independent statistical observations or a balanced index of overall diplomatic alignment. Direct first-person reaffirmations of support, opposition and commitment are also recognized; reaffirming a commitment to reject or weaken a different object is withheld. Detection is automatic and provisional; source affiliations and wording are not independently verified.

Validation covers each addition's support/opposition, conditional and reported wording, wrong objects, automatic discovery, source-linked rendering, no-imputation baseline, collective abstention and duplicate-invariant weighting. Cross-question and regional boundary tests exercise likely conflations. Browser tests cover the new debt question and policy-area metadata, exports, desktop/mobile layout, filter restoration and exact report reproduction after saving and reopening. Retained-source replay is private; all 37 reserved meetings remain unopened and no external AI calls are used.
