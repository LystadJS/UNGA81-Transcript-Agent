# Browser release 1.30.0

Imported collections may carry documentary policy review notes separately from UN transcript evidence. The report and script-free HTML show dated official documents, source-bound national transcript references, signed date differences, relationships and qualifications. This is imported assistant-provisional interpretation, not automatic government-policy classification or a current legal-effect certification.

The browser validates bounded metadata, official HTTPS source hosts, actual calendar dates and an eligible national transcript anchor with matching identifier, date and text hash. Filtered, unresolved, collective or changed sources withhold the comparison. Originals remain saved. Documentary notes never enter UN statement counts, nearby-date findings, country trajectories or agreement scores. No government document is downloaded by the app.

Validation: synthetic source-binding, filtering, unsafe-link/date/attribution abstention and HTML escaping; browser save/reopen of four documentary items, identical filters/results, script-free export and mobile/desktop layout. Real source documents and review notes remain private collection data. No reserved meetings opened; publication gates unchanged.
