How to review · scope and safeguards

One source segment, one decision

Read the original text and neighboring segments. Choose the source type and speech extent. An automatic source segment may contain only part of a speech—or more than one speaker.

Complete means the observed segment or explicitly linked segments support a complete intervention. Near-complete requires a note describing the gap. Both require an explicit speech ID. Use Fragment or Unknown when that claim is not supported.

Do not repair evidence here

For mixed speakers or an internal split, choose Uncertain / internal boundary problem and explain the required split in notes. Original text, source metadata and country attribution cannot be edited. Technical token windows are not semantic topic boundaries.

“Confirmed” means you made a review decision, not that the passage is necessarily eligible for modeling. Previous owner pilot decisions are separate and unchanged.

Changes are cached locally where the browser permits it. Save review JSON is the durable copy. A downloaded partial review can be resumed. No transcript or review data are uploaded; original UN links open only when clicked.
