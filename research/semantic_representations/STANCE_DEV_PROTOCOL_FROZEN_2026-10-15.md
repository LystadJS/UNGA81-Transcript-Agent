# Prospective development-only stance-sensitive comparison — protocol freeze

**Freeze status:** This document is a prospective design specification. Do not interpret the Git commit timestamp as experimental blinding. Execute the new benchmark only after this design has been committed to the worker branch, and retain every failure. No restricted transcripts, real UN corpus, archival lexical fit or sealed October 2026 holdout may be read.

## Aim and fixed evaluation population

Use exactly **30 newly authored synthetic proposition families**, four sentences each (anchor, topical paraphrase, contradictory proposition, protocol-prefixed counterstatement): **120 observations**. Preserve the earlier 16-family/64-observation benchmark as a separate failed development record, **not** for model/threshold selection. New families are fixed by this design to strata: **8 explicit first-person support/opposition**, **6 grammatically negated first-person stance cues**, **4 scope/conditional qualification**, **4 quoted or third-party attribution**, **4 changed object/proposition scope**, **4 cue-free/ambiguous expressions**. These are purposive English, not probability-sampled or an independent holdout. The sole supported substantive judgment for each sentence is its authored proposition-scoped relation, not verified government stance.

Use identical UTF-8 text, hashes, IDs, synthetic split and source-selection denominator for all representations. A family has four entries; the complete collection has 30 families / 120 rows. Missing encodings, skipped cases and partial runs remain explicitly counted; never normalize rates over successful encodings alone. No researcher may change the fixture, thresholds or candidate method rules after seeing execution outcomes without a *new* named exploratory run and freeze.

## Fixed methods

- **M0 pretrained topical geometry:** existing fully pinned read-only MiniLM-L6-v2 ONNX model, exact source checkpoint revision and SHA-256 manifest, 256-token context with original WordPiece-safe segmentation, cosine similarity.
- **L0 transparent lexical comparator:** word (1,2)-gram TF-IDF (scikit-learn 1.8.0), fit **once** to the same 120 authored synthetic strings, lowercase, L2 normalization. This is an expressly development-fitted source-matched lexical comparator, **not** the frozen historical TF-IDF/LSA reference fit.
- **S1 polarity-gated MiniLM geometry:** MiniLM cosine combined with deterministic first-person/explicit polarity extraction, and a strict, predeclared *synthetic proposition-id equality* requirement. When both row polarities are unambiguously observed and their topic/proposition IDs match, rank opposite polarity below matching polarity. Otherwise **abstain**. No new pretrained stance checkpoint and no semantic claim without independently verified proposition matching.
- **S2 polarity-augmented lexical geometry:** same source-matched TF-IDF with a fixed signed polarity coordinate of magnitude **2** for unambiguous scoped cases (the value is a prospective design constant, **not fitted**); abstain otherwise. Compare L2-normalized augmented vectors with cosine. This tests a compositional symbolic direction feature, **not a validated general stance model**.
- **C0 explicit cue diagnostic:** support/endorse/welcome/favor versus oppose/reject cues, simple unambiguous immediate `not`/`do not` cue inversion, first-person authority; reject quoted/third-party, `unless`, `only if`, `provided that`, and cue-free ambiguous cases. This is a handcrafted diagnostic and shares rules with S1/S2. It cannot count as independent method corroboration.

Explicit-scope inference is forbidden for quotes, third-party attribution, conditional language, ambiguous negation, unsupported actor/object linkage, cross-proposition pairs and cue-free text. Absence of cues must yield **ABSTAIN**, not a negative stance. The predeclared rules should reject ambiguous cases rather than manufacture positives. All representation candidates are engineering-only; the existing full scientific and public release gate remains closed.

## Fixed outcomes, denominators and decision thresholds

Report every stratum separately and the whole 30-family population. For the **14 explicit/negated cue families**, evaluate whether anchor→paraphrase similarity exceeds anchor→contradiction similarity under M0/L0/S1/S2; ties are counted as failures. Also report each protocol-contamination cosine without treating similarity as agreement. On the **16 guard families**, require S1/S2 to abstain on all three attempted anchor comparisons, with the original entire family-level denominator retained (16 families / 48 pair comparisons). Any unsupported confident output is a safety failure.

**Advance-to-new-independent-synthetic-holdout criterion (not release):** S1 or S2 must obtain at least 13/14 correct ordered comparisons among the 14 cue families, abstain on at least 15/16 guard families, have 0 uncovered or silent failed inference batches, and have 100% duplicate-source/tampered-input refusal in their new adversarial tests. A candidate meeting these *development* thresholds merits only a **prospectively selected, genuinely independent synthetic holdout** with newly authored cases and external labels. It does not merit opening the existing reserved October 2026 texts. Even a PASS is not validation of general diplomatic stance accuracy. No post-hoc threshold tuning or candidate ranking by attractive charts.

Negative controls: contradiction vs paraphrase with shared issue words; quoted and third-party stances; changed proposition-object scope; conditional exceptions; missing/ambiguous cues; source identity tampering; Unicode and complete chunk coverage; corrupt artifact hashes; deterministic fresh-process replay. At least 30 families, 120 texts and both independent-process attempts must appear in the final receipt. Compare against original 16-family adverse record without replacing it.
