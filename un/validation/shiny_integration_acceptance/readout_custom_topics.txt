BOTSWANA
Speaker: Duma Gideon Boko | Title: President
Date: 23 September 2026 | Region: Africa
- UN reform. Supported UN80 but argued that efficiency savings should strengthen conflict prevention and delivery in program countries. Called for Security Council reform consistent with the common African position, including permanent representation for Africa and Latin America.
