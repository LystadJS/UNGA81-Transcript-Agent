METHOD AND EVIDENCE CONTRACT

Limits of interpretation

Stability is not significance. Language similarities and cluster labels do not establish government positions, alliances, or causal influence. Recorded source affiliations are not verified speaker identities. W3 diffusion coordinates describe geometric random walks on a constructed graph, not policy transmission.

Private development-source evaluation, held-out validation, source-byte independent checks, and model-weight downloads are not part of this public synthetic browser preview.

Experimental research, not a released diplomatic analysis · Source-bound inputs only
