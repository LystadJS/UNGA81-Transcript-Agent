What the exports contain

Downloads from one prepared report share a timestamp in their filenames, so you can keep the files together.

HTML is a standalone, script-free report suitable for printing, with the chosen appendix and your separately labeled notes. JSON preserves the original collection, source hashes, meeting and appendix selections, passage notes and mission preferences so the report can be rebuilt here. CSV contains all source passages from the selected meetings, independently of search and appendix selection; it excludes your notes. Potential spreadsheet formulas are prefixed with an apostrophe; JSON retains exact text.

Each download, including the complete ZIP, is limited to 25 MB; oversized output is refused without truncation. These files can contain transcript text and your notes. Nothing is uploaded or saved automatically. Save JSON or a report package before closing. Extract the package and open report.json to resume editing. The maintained archive and research CSV pipeline remain separate.
