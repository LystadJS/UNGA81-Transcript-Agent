How it works

  1. Get a review packet from the coordinator and open it above. Packet files are not published on this site.
  2. Check the source context and intervention type, then choose a relevance label. Keep test labels out of training and tuning.
  3. Prepare and return the completed review. The coordinator validates its packet identity and imports it into the project.

This is a static review and reporting interface. Running new pipeline analyses, model training and Shiny still requires the project’s computing environment. Shared accounts, centralized storage and automatic submission require a separate authenticated service.

Access depends on your organization allowing GitHub Pages and the required browser features. This site does not bypass device or network policies. If downloads are blocked, use the copy/paste options and your approved collaboration channel.
