About the dataCoverage, interpretation and privacy

The report describes the transcripts collected for your query. Each passage follows the source’s segment boundaries and may include procedural remarks. English transcripts can contain transcription or interpretation errors; check quotations against the source.

A phrase match does not establish a speaker’s position. Counts and text comparisons describe language in the collected material. Missing transcripts and unknown affiliations remain identified in the report.

Your files and review choices stay on your device. Live collection contacts the UN transcript service. Download completed reviews and return them to your coordinator; the site does not submit them.
