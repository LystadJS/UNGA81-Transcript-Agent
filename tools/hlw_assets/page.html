04 / COVERAGE & METHODS

What these results can—and cannot—show

Published inventory, not a speech census

170 of 226 indexed meetings were downloadable: six General Debate meetings, 131 broader-week candidates and 33 background proceedings. Venue and formal HLW membership were not independently reconciled. The 56 empty transcript pages remain in the inventory; alternative-source recovery and overlapping recordings are tracked separately.

Automatic transcripts

163 downloads identify English, six floor-language and one French. No translation or multilingual recall is claimed. The source flags timestamps in 59 transcripts as unreliable; time links are navigation aids.

Exact evidence, limited inference

Every extracted quotation was checked against source text. Introductions and procedural references remain candidates. Keyword matching does not establish stance, legal status or diplomatic alignment.

Full text preserved

11,146 nonempty segments became 3,728 grouped upload records across three batches; two duplicates were excluded from matching. No text was silently sampled. The upload workflow did not execute the full D1 daily adapters or release research models.
